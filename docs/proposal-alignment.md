## Current Azure baseline checked read-only

The initial read-only inspection found a `CloudInABox` management group with a child `Tenant` group, an enabled platform subscription, and an MCA invoice section available to the operator. After that inspection, the UK South policy assignment, worker roles, and a separate `vnet-cib-hub` (`10.255.0.0/16`) were deployed. Firewall and VPN Gateway remain disabled. Existing tenant VNets and resource groups in the platform subscription are legacy shared-subscription deployments; they were not migrated. No new tenant subscription has yet been vended as an end-to-end test.

## Evaluation evidence

`GET /admin/evaluation/evidence.csv` exports stored request and verification measurements. Fields requiring external measurement, such as manual baseline time, survey score, or cross-tenant traffic findings, are intentionally left empty. Report the sample size, task script, participant consent process, data handling, and limitations alongside any future results. Do not infer study outcomes from the presence of the export endpoint.
