## Invitation and purpose

You are invited to take part in a study evaluating how understandable and usable the Cloud-in-a-Box portal is for requesting and reviewing a cloud environment. The study evaluates the software and its workflow, not your ability. Taking part is voluntary.

## What participation involves

You will be asked to complete the tasks in the approved task sheet. The session is expected to take about `[duration]` minutes. The researcher may record task completion, elapsed time, observed difficulties, and responses to the approved questionnaire. Screen/audio recording will occur only if it is separately approved and you agree below.

## Risks and costs

The study should use fictional details and a designated test environment. Do not enter your own passwords, Azure credentials, business data, or personal customer information. A live Azure provisioning test may create billable cloud resources; the researcher is responsible for the test environment and its cleanup under the approved plan. Azure budget alerts do not cap spending. Tell the researcher if you see information that appears to belong to another person or organization.
